! Instagram exposes no network-level safe mode, so the unfiltered discovery
! entry points are hidden and the request policy blocks their routes. Feed,
! profiles, stories, messages and search remain reachable.

! be hidden or search disappears with it. Its navigation is redirected to the
! keyword search page instead. Only Reels and the people/location browsers are
! hidden here.
